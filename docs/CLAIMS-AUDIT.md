## A warning that applies to checking this document

Search engines' AI summaries confidently restate mmakf.in's own claims — "WKF affiliated",
"Limca Book of Records", "130+ schools" — as though independently established. Every underlying
link is mmakf.in itself. **That echo is not corroboration.** Anyone spot-checking this report with
search snippets will be told the claims are confirmed when they are only being repeated back.

## 1. The WKF affiliation claim — REQUIRES DECISION, and it is urgent

**What the site says**, in at least six places: "WKF International Pathway"; a headline statistic
reading "WKF"; "formally registered under WKF SportsID and Sportdata"; and an FAQ answering "Are
MMAKF belts and certificates recognised?" with "Yes."

**What was established:**

- The World Karate Federation recognises **exactly one national federation per country**.
- For India, that body is the **Karate India Organisation (KIO)**, Janakpuri, New Delhi.
- WKF de-recognised the Karate Association of India on 22 June 2020 and permanently disaffiliated
  it on 15 November 2021 in Dubai, granting KIO permanent recognition the same day.
- Therefore **MMAKF cannot itself hold WKF affiliation**, and no body other than KIO can.
- MMAKF does not appear in the Jharkhand Olympic Association's list of 36 affiliated state
  associations. The karate body listed there is the Sports Karate Association of Jharkhand.
- **The strongest physical evidence points elsewhere**: the grading certificates visible in
  MMAKF's own ceremony photographs are legible, and their letterhead reads
  **"KARATE FEDERATION OF SHOTOKAN – INDIA"** — a different awarding body from the one the site
  names. A championship banner in another photograph shows WSMAC and AIKF crests, and no WKF crest.

**Why this matters more than any other item here.** It is the first thing a sceptical parent,
state unit or ministry official checks, and it is the one claim that cannot be engineered away.

**The legitimate route exists and is worth stating accurately:** dojo → a KIO-affiliated Jharkhand
state association → KIO → WKF. Describing MMAKF as working *towards* that pathway is defensible.
Describing it as *holding* WKF standing is not.

**Decision needed:** either supply the evidence, or the wording is corrected to describe the
pathway as an objective rather than a status. Until you decide, the claim stays as you wrote it —
this is your federation's statement about itself, not mine to delete.

## 2. "Tiger Lee Lineage" — this is a real achievement, described wrongly

**What the site says:** "Jr. Tiger Lee Lineage", presented as a Shotokan transmission of teaching.

**What was established:**

- **No karate teacher named "Tiger Lee" is documented anywhere.** All search hits are unrelated US
  taekwondo schools. Won-kuk Lee (who did train under Gichin and Gigō Funakoshi) has no "Tiger"
  nickname and no India connection; the recognised "Tiger of Shotokan" is Keinosuke Enoeda, who has
  no India link.
- **"Junior Tiger Lee" (जूनियर टाइगर ली) is a TITLE CONFERRED ON Shihan Pramod Kumar Pathak in
  2021** — not a lineage he inherited.
- It is corroborated by *Shivangan Publication's Jharkhand Current Affairs 2022*, whose quiz
  question asks which martial-arts coach of Jharkhand was given the title "Junior Tiger Lee", with
  him as the answer.

**This is the single strongest independently-corroborated fact in the entire dossier** — the only
third-party publication that confirms anything. It is being wasted by being described as something
it is not. "Awarded the title *Junior Tiger Lee*, 2021" is both true and more impressive than a
lineage nobody can trace.

**Recommendation:** restate it as a conferred honour. **REQUIRES YOUR CONFIRMATION.**

## 3. Titles — three sources, three different answers

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| mmakf.in | "Shihan · Grandmaster · Soke", **VI Dan** |
| His own YouTube channel and the former MMAKF website | **"Renshi"** |
| A recovered newspaper clipping | Awarded **Black Belt 5th Dan in 2022** |

**Renshi, Shihan and Soke are three different things.** Renshi is a teaching title; Shihan denotes
a senior instructor; Soke means the head and founder of a style. A V→VI Dan promotion after 2022 is
entirely possible, but nothing evidences it.

The same contradiction exists for Sensei Sumitra: the site says "Sumitra Devi, III Dan"; the former
site says "Sensei Sumitra Pathak"; both newspapers call her "सुमित्रा, ब्लैक बेल्ट सेकंड डॉन" (2nd Dan).

**Decision needed:** pick one form for each person and state when and by whom it was awarded. A
federation that cannot state its own leaders' grades consistently undermines every certificate it
issues.

## 4. Limca Book of Records / Guinness World Records — REQUIRES DECISION

**These are not inventions of the website.** They trace to specific Hindi newspaper clippings, now
recovered and self-hosted at `/media/archive/press-records-clipping.jpg`, which report:

- breaking 25 hockey sticks on his body (timed at 23 seconds in two clippings, 25 in another),
  described as a Limca Book of Records entry;
- holding two 407 trucks pulling in opposite directions for 1 minute 50 seconds, described as
  "given a place in the Guinness Book of World Records".

**But that is local press reporting the subject's own account**, and the scans were self-hosted on
his own former website. There is **no independent verification in either direction**. Limca has no
publicly searchable database, so this cannot be checked online by anyone.

**Decision needed:** if MMAKF holds a certificate, letter or entry number from either body, it can
be published and the claim becomes verifiable. If not, the honest form is to attribute it — "as
reported by *[outlet]*, *[date]*" — rather than asserting it as established fact.

## 5. Statistics — unverified

"5,000+ students per quarter", "130+ schools reached", "34 active black belts". No independent
confirmation of any of the three. These are internal figures; they are not implausible, but the
site presents them as headline facts.

**Recommendation:** keep them, but label them as federation records and date them ("as at
[month/year]"). An undated figure quietly becomes untrue.

## 6. Legal registration — NOT established, and must not be reported as absent

No society registration, trust deed, Section 8 company, CIN, PAN or GST entry was found for
"Modern Martial Arts Karate-Do Federation of India".

**This must NOT be read as "MMAKF is unregistered."** The searches were incomplete: ZaubaCorp and
Trademarkia returned HTTP 403, the NGO Darpan interface is JavaScript-driven and could not be
queried, and Jharkhand's society register is not publicly searchable.

**This matters practically, not just reputationally:** a payment gateway will require the
registration certificate, PAN and bank details of a registered entity before it will onboard MMAKF
as a merchant. You cannot collect fees institutionally without it.

**Action needed:** supply the registration number and certificate. It should be published on the
site — a body whose legal status can be checked is a body.

## 7. Press coverage — real, and it was invisible

**Five genuine Hindi newspaper clippings exist.** They have been recovered and are now self-hosted
under `/media/archive/`, because they survived only on the CDN of MMAKF's own lapsed predecessor
site, `modernmartialartpatratu.com`, and were one Wix account expiry from being lost entirely.

| Outlet | Date | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| Jharkhand Prahari | Nov 2023 | Belt grading at Rasda, ~45 students; names Sensei Vikas Pathak as chief instructor |
| Jharkhand Ujala | Nov 2023 | The same grading, independently covered |
| Johar Jharkhand | Aug 2022 | Bharat Gaurav Karate Khel Ratna award |
| Sandhya Prahari | Aug 2022 | The "Junior Tiger Lee" title |
| Masthead cropped | Undated | The record attempts |

**However:** in the searchable, indexed press there is **zero** coverage. Three researchers
searched independently in Hindi and English and found nothing on Dainik Jagran, Prabhat Khabar,
Livehindustan or Dainik Bhaskar. This is not for want of appetite — Prabhat Khabar covers Ramgarh
district karate heavily — but that coverage belongs to other bodies.

One genuine gap remains: jagran.com, livehindustan.com and bhaskar.com block automated access and
their site search is client-side, so three of six target outlets are genuinely unsearched. A human
with a browser and their Ramgarh e-paper archives could still find more.

## 8. Photography — fixed, with one deliberate omission

The site illustrated **named people and named events with stock photographs from Unsplash**. A news
item headlined "Sensei Vikas Pathak elevated to IV Dan" carried a generic photograph of an
unrelated person exercising.

**What was done:**

- The championship item now carries a **real MMAKF medal ceremony**.
- The women's self-defence item now carries a **real MMAKF school class**.
- Gallery tiles now use **real grading and dojo photographs**.
- **The Vikas Pathak item now carries NO photograph at all.**

That last one is deliberate. **No captioned photograph of Sensei Vikas Pathak exists anywhere
publicly.** Two candidate images were found and neither is captioned. Publishing either under his
name would replace stock fakery with misattribution — the same error in better clothing. **He must
supply a photograph**, and then it goes in.

Photographs of Shihan Pramod Kumar Pathak **do** exist and are now held locally, including one
captioned with his name in a newspaper — the only name-to-face link that exists anywhere.

**Still needed from you:** a portrait photograph of each named instructor. There are 51 further
real photographs still on the archived CDN that have not been mined.

## 9. The largest asset, which the website was hiding

**mmakf.in contained ZERO outbound links.** Its only links were a `mailto:` and a `tel:`.

Meanwhile the federation operates a YouTube channel — **"Pramod Pathak Martial Arts Academy"**
(`@PramodPathakMartialArt`, channel ID `UCGb5ET3JdoCUdNNCqekYJ1g`) — with thousands of videos,
posting daily, and an audience far larger than the website's. **That channel links to mmakf.in. The
website linked back to nothing.**

That single omission is why none of the federation's real material ever surfaced alongside its
site. **Now fixed:** every verified channel is linked in the footer of every page and declared in
the site's structured data, so search engines connect them.

Verified live before publication: two YouTube channels, Facebook, X, and a Telegram channel.

**One unresolved:** Instagram. Two researchers reported fetching `@mmakf_india`; a third
demonstrated that Instagram serves an identical login wall for every handle including invented
ones, so those details cannot have been legitimately obtained. Note the official handle in the
former site's own social bar is `@mmak_india`, not `@mmakf_india`. **Confirm which account is
yours** and it will be added.
